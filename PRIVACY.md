# Reef Glass — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 4, 2026*

Reef Glass is a screensaver for Roku devices. It does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

- **No network access.** Reef Glass makes no network requests. All of its images and code ship inside the package.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking.** Reef Glass contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- **One setting saved, on the device.** Reef Glass stores only your lighting choice (follow the clock, daytime, dusk or night) on your Roku. It reads the device's clock only to match the tank's lighting to the time of day.
- **No accounts and no purchases.**

Roku itself may collect information about your use of its devices and the Roku Channel Store under [Roku's privacy policy](https://docs.roku.com/published/userprivacypolicy/en/us). Reef Glass has no access to that information.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/timsaucer/reef_glass_roku_screensaver/issues.
