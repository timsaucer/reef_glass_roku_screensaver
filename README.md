# Reef Glass

A Roku screensaver: a calm saltwater reef tank seen full screen through the glass. A photoreal
plate of rock, coral and sand, with fish, critters and light animated over it.

Status: **photoreal art in place** for the plate and all animals.

## What's in the tank

| | |
|---|---|
| Fish | clownfish pair living in an anemone (part of the plate): the smaller male follows the female as she patrols it, hovers over it shuffling and turning in place, and now and then darts a short way out and back, blue tangs and yellow tangs cruising the whole tank, royal grammas under ledges, a loose group of blue damselfish around a coral head |
| Critters | feather duster worms that snap shut when a fish swims close and slowly unfurl, a giant clam, a starfish crawling across the sand |
| Moray eel | a snowflake moray lives in the cave under the right-hand rock: it breathes with a slow gape, slides out to look around, and pulls back into the dark when a big fish swims close |
| Swimming | each fish is a 3D model (reconstructed from its photo), drawn from 26 pre-rendered views (snapping to the nearest, never blending two), with three strengths of body bend through the turn, so it turns with real thickness and shows its face head-on; fish swim at any angle between side-on and nearly head-on, visibly coming toward the glass and heading off into the distance; depth changes across the reef happen only over open water, so fish never pop from behind the coral to in front of it |
| Light | caustics rippling over the sand, shafts of light from the surface, drifting marine snow |
| Time of day | follows the Roku's clock: daylight, a blue dusk and dawn, dim moonlight at night (fish slow down, caustics and light shafts fade out) |

There is no settings screen.

## Layout

- `roku/reefglass/`: the screensaver package (BrightScript, `roScreen`, 30 fps cap)
  - `source/main.brs`: entry point, frame loop, memory monitoring, helpers
  - `source/reef.brs`: draw order, light (caustics, rays, day/night), marine snow
  - `source/fish.brs`: fish behavior, swim/turn animation, depth haze
  - `source/critters.brs`: anemone, feather dusters, starfish
  - `source/moray.brs`: the moray eel in the cave
  - `source/dev.brs`: dev-only `Main` with launch parameters (see below)
  - `scenes/reef.json`: every position and tuning value
  - `images/`: built assets (generated by `tools/`; do not edit by hand)
- `tools/`: asset pipeline and device helpers
- `gen/prompts/`, `gen/drafts/`: image prompts and kept generations

## Art contract

Final art must keep these conventions so it drops in without code changes. Positions live in
`scenes/reef.json`; sprite sizes are read from the files.

| File | Content | Anchor / notes |
|---|---|---|
| `bg.jpg` | 1920×1080 opaque plate: rock, coral, sand, water. | no fish, no critters that move |
| `fg.png` | near rocks/coral in front of most fish | full-frame RGBA |
| `rocks_low.png` | plate's non-sand pixels from `caustics.top` down | drawn over the caustics so light lands on sand only |
| `<fish>_atlas<p>.png` | the fish's body and tail sprites at every heading of `views.deg` (-60 to 90 degrees; the rest are mirrors), straight and bent, packed into atlas pages | per view in `views.v` and `views.bend.sets`: fish center in the body sprite, in the tail sprite, tail joint relative to the center, then each sprite's atlas page and rectangle |
| `duster_crown.png`, `duster_tube.png` | feather duster fan; tube | crown's bottom center sits on the tube mouth |
| `starfish.png` | starfish, seen at a low angle | centered |
| `moray.png`, `moray_jaw.png`, `cave_shade.png` | eel head and front body facing left, without its lower jaw; the lower jaw; the cave's dark interior (alpha rising toward the back) | `moray.x,y` left middle when fully out; jaw hinged at `moray.jawHinge`; shade at `moray.shade` |
| `caustic_<i>.png`, `ray.png`, `mote.png`, `puff.png` | procedural (`tools/caustics.py`, `tools/placeholders.py`) | |

## Device workflow

`.env` (gitignored) holds `ROKU_IP`, `ROKU_DEV_PASSWORD`, `ROKU_DEV_ID`, `ROKU_SIGN_PASSWORD`.

- `tools/run.sh [secs] [query]`: sideload the dev build, launch it, capture the console, screenshot to `build/shot.jpg`
- launch parameters: `hour=<h>` pin the clock (e.g. `hour=23`), `daycycle=<s>` sweep a whole day in s seconds, `profile=1` print timings and critter states, `trace=<species>` print one fish's motion twice a second, `pose=<species>` draw that fish bent through a U-turn in 9 steps (`fine=1`: 45 to 90 degrees only)
- `tools/sideload.sh roku/reefglass [--release]`, `tools/console.py <secs> [--launch]`, `tools/screenshot.sh out.jpg`
- `tools/placeholders.py`: regenerate placeholder art (overwrites real art; for bootstrapping only)

## Art pipeline

1. `tools/gen.sh <prompt> <seed>...` (set `W`/`H`): one Z-Image-Turbo draft per seed, strictly
   one at a time.
2. Plate: crop to 16:9, `tools/plate.py grid` to read coordinates, trace `gen/plate_spec.json`,
   `tools/plate.py build`; `tools/depth.py` + `tools/layers.py --near 0.08 --matte` for the reef
   layer (quantize into `fg.png`, alpha remapped `clip((a - 0.05) / 0.5)` so the coral fully hides fish behind it); `tools/fx.py` for the caustic band height.
   `tools/coralmask.py` then writes the coarse coral map (`coral` in reef.json) from `fg.png`.
3. Animals: drafts on a plain green background; `tools/cutout.py <draft> <name> --width <px>`
   keys and despills them.
4. Fish views (TripoSR, in its own env; see below): key the fish whole with
   `tools/cutout.py <draft> <name>_full --width 600` (kept in `gen/meshes/`), reconstruct it with
   `ext/venv/bin/python tools/fish3d.py gen/meshes/<name>_full.png gen/meshes/<name>.ply`, then
   `ext/venv/bin/python tools/fishviews.py views gen/meshes/<name>.ply <name> --photo
   gen/meshes/<name>_full.png [--fat 1.25]` renders the turn views (the photo is projected onto
   both flanks; `--fat` thickens thin fish a little). `fishviews.py strip` previews a turn. The moray: `tools/moray.py`.
5. `tools/icons.py <screenshot>` for icons and the store poster.

TripoSR setup (not committed; `ext/` is gitignored):
`git clone https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSR ext/TripoSR`, `uv venv -p 3.11 ext/venv`,
`VIRTUAL_ENV=ext/venv uv pip install torch omegaconf einops transformers==4.44.2 trimesh
huggingface-hub scikit-image pillow moderngl numpy imageio imagequant`. Two local patches: in
`tsr/models/isosurface.py` marching cubes comes from scikit-image instead of torchmcubes
(returning vertices in torchmcubes' z, y, x order), and in `tsr/utils.py` the `rembg` import is
optional. The weights (`stabilityai/TripoSR`) download on first use.
