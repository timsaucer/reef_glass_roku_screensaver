# Reef Glass

A Roku screensaver: a calm saltwater reef tank seen full screen through the glass. A photoreal
plate of rock, coral and sand, with fish, critters and light animated over it.

Status: **core animation built with placeholder art**; photoreal art not generated yet.

## What's in the tank

| | |
|---|---|
| Fish | clownfish pair living in an anemone, blue tangs and yellow tangs cruising the whole tank, royal grammas under ledges, a loose group of blue damselfish around a coral head, a flame angel |
| Critters | feather duster worms that snap shut when a fish swims close and slowly unfurl, a giant clam with a shimmering mantle, a starfish crawling across the sand |
| Goby and pistol shrimp | a watchman goby guards a burrow while a pistol shrimp bulldozes sand out of it; when a big fish grazes over the sand both dive in, and the goby peeks back out once it's calm |
| Light | caustics rippling over the sand, shafts of light from the surface, drifting marine snow |
| Time of day | follows the Roku's clock: daylight, a blue dusk and dawn, dim moonlight at night (fish slow down, caustics and light shafts fade out) |

There is no settings screen.

## Layout

- `roku/reefglass/`: the screensaver package (BrightScript, `roScreen`, 30 fps cap)
  - `source/main.brs`: entry point, frame loop, memory monitoring, helpers
  - `source/reef.brs`: draw order, light (caustics, rays, day/night), marine snow
  - `source/fish.brs`: fish behavior, swim/turn animation, depth haze
  - `source/critters.brs`: anemone, feather dusters, clam, starfish, goby and shrimp burrow
  - `source/dev.brs`: dev-only `Main` with launch parameters (see below)
  - `scenes/reef.json`: every position and tuning value
  - `images/`: built assets (generated by `tools/`; do not edit by hand)
- `tools/`: asset pipeline and device helpers
- `gen/prompts/`, `gen/drafts/`: image prompts and kept generations

## Art contract

Final art must keep these conventions so it drops in without code changes. Positions live in
`scenes/reef.json`; sprite sizes are read from the files.

| File | Content | Anchor / notes |
|---|---|---|
| `bg.jpg` | 1920×1080 opaque plate: rock, coral, sand, water. Burrow mouth on open sand. | no fish, no critters that move |
| `fg.png` | near rocks/coral in front of most fish | full-frame RGBA |
| `rocks_low.png` | plate's non-sand pixels from `caustics.top` down | drawn over the caustics so light lands on sand only |
| `<fish>_body.png` | fish facing right, tail cut off | `pivot` in reef.json: where the tail joins, body pixels |
| `<fish>_tail.png` | tail fin alone | its right-edge middle attaches at the pivot |
| `anemone_<n>.png` | anemone sway loop, n frames | bottom center at `anemone.x,y` |
| `duster_crown.png`, `duster_tube.png` | feather duster fan; tube | crown's bottom center sits on the tube mouth |
| `clam.png`, `clam_shimmer.png` | clam; mantle highlights (alpha) | bottom center at `clam.x,y` |
| `starfish.png` | starfish, seen at a low angle | centered |
| `goby_body/tail.png`, `shrimp.png`, `lip.png` | goby, pistol shrimp facing right, front rim of the burrow mouth | lip drawn over both so they sink into the hole |
| `caustic_<i>.png`, `ray.png`, `mote.png`, `puff.png` | procedural (`tools/caustics.py`, `tools/placeholders.py`) | |

## Device workflow

`.env` (gitignored) holds `ROKU_IP`, `ROKU_DEV_PASSWORD`, `ROKU_DEV_ID`, `ROKU_SIGN_PASSWORD`.

- `tools/run.sh [secs] [query]`: sideload the dev build, launch it, capture the console, screenshot to `build/shot.jpg`
- launch parameters: `hour=<h>` pin the clock (e.g. `hour=23`), `daycycle=<s>` sweep a whole day in s seconds, `profile=1` print timings and critter states
- `tools/sideload.sh roku/reefglass [--release]`, `tools/console.py <secs> [--launch]`, `tools/screenshot.sh out.jpg`
- `tools/placeholders.py`: regenerate placeholder art
